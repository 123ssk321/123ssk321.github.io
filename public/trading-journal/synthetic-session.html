Decisions2

Recorded strategy/person choices, including hold/no-execution decisions.

Trade record groups1

Distinct source + run + supplied trade identities. Includes unexecuted intents and unlinked legacy groups; not a count of known round trips.

Orders2

Distinct known order identities. Lifecycle callbacks do not add orders; missing IDs are excluded.

Executed linked intents1

Trade identities with a fill linked to a known order. Unlinked legacy fill groups are excluded. This is not a round-trip count.

Fills3

Individual executions. Two partial fills on one order count as two fills, one order.

Position close records1

Explicit source close callbacks only. Imported segments may omit an earlier entry or later exit.

Reason evidence2 / 2

Decisions with a recorded contemporaneous reason / all recorded decisions. Unknown reasons remain in the denominator.

Market context2 / 2

Decisions with context / all recorded decisions. Zero decisions means coverage is unavailable, not 0%.

Unlinked fills0

Recorded executions without native order/trade linkage. Kept as separate evidence groups; their entry/exit reasons and round trips are unknown.

Records are grouped by explicit trade identity, not guessed buy/sell pairs. A partial fill does not create another trade. Legacy reports can have fills without linked reasons; their reasons cannot be reconstructed honestly.
