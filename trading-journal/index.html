What the journal can actually tell us.

Decision evidence, execution coverage and the gaps between them. This page contains bounded aggregates for one selected report. Full journals stay local.

Trade identities, at least

Distinct recorded intents. Includes choices that never became executions; this is not completed trades.

Evidence records, at least

Decisions, order callbacks, fills and later outcomes. Several records can describe one trade.

Decision reasons recorded

Trade intents whose every recorded decision has a known reason / intents with decisions. Rounded down to ten percentage points.

Decision context recorded

Same denominator as reasons, requiring context for every recorded decision. Unknown evidence reduces coverage.

How to read these numbers

A plus sign means a privacy-rounded lower bound, not an exact count. For example, 40+ means at least 40 recorded identities. A 70% reason figure is a rounded coverage bucket, not a win rate or confidence score. Bars count the selected record type or trade intent mode; their heights are not returns.

Missing evidence matters

Counts are rounded down to tens. Fewer than 20 trade intents suppress all statistics. Distributions with any cell below five are suppressed in full. Evidence coverage is rounded down to ten percentage points. No returns or profitability claims are published. Trade intents include unexecuted decisions; missing records do not prove an event did not occur.

Known coverage measures whether every recorded decision for a trade intent has contemporaneous evidence. Unknown evidence remains unknown. This is a journaling demonstration, not a strategy recommendation.
